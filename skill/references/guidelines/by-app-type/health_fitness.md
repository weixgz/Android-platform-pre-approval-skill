# Health And Fitness Apps

Use this guide for medical, health-management, wellness, step-tracking, appointment, or health-commerce apps.

## Higher-scrutiny areas

Check:

- health-related claims implied by the app name, strings, or graphics
- whether camera, microphone, location, and phone-state permissions are clearly tied to user-facing health features
- whether the privacy prompt appears before sensitive flows

## Risk patterns

Flag:

- heavy data access with weak privacy explanation
- ambiguous wording that makes the app look like a medical service without clear supporting materials
- test-domain privacy policy links in a health-related app

## Manual verification prompts

Ask for manual confirmation of:

- privacy-policy ownership and medical/health disclosure wording
- complaint, contact, or rights-management channels
- account deletion or data-deletion path
- whether the store listing over-claims diagnosis, treatment, or regulated medical capability
