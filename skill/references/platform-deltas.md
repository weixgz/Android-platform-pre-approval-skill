# Store-specific deltas

Apply these deltas after the domestic baseline.

## Google Play

Focus on:

- sensitive permissions and special access matching the promoted core feature
- package-install, overlay, accessibility, or other high-scrutiny capability justification
- cleartext traffic and risky update behavior

## HUAWEI AppGallery

Focus on:

- privacy-policy URL readiness
- stronger explanation materials for heavy permissions
- consistency between package behavior and listing story

## Xiaomi

Focus on:

- contextual permission requests
- graceful denial handling
- proof for heavy or unusual permission declarations

## Yingyongbao

Focus on:

- privacy-policy主体 consistency
- package evidence supporting the declared business purpose
- caution around heavy permissions in consumer apps

## vivo

Focus on:

- conservative privacy review
- runtime and storefront consistency
- visible rights-management and permission explanation

## OPPO

Focus on:

- minimum-necessary permissions
- privacy-policy URL quality
- strict review of package-install, overlay, accessibility, device-admin, and broad visibility capabilities
