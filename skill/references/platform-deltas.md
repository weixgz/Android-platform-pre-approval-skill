# Store-specific deltas

Apply these deltas after the domestic baseline.

## Google Play

Focus on:
- sensitive permissions and special access matching the app's promoted core functionality
- device and network abuse signals such as silent install, self-update, dynamic code loading, or deceptive update prompts
- transparency gaps when the APK requests sensitive access but shows weak privacy, consent, or deletion signals

## HUAWEI AppGallery

Focus on:
- whether submission materials are likely to include a privacy policy URL
- whether app identity, listing text, and package behavior could drift apart
- whether sensitive permissions or risky capabilities will need stronger review explanation and screenshots

## Xiaomi

Focus on:
- dynamic, scenario-based permission requests instead of one-time blanket prompts
- whether denial of a permission appears likely to block registration, login, or basic use
- whether high-scrutiny permissions appear to need separate declaration materials or proof artifacts

## Yingyongbao

Focus on:
- privacy-policy accessibility and consistency between developer entity, privacy-policy主体, and app behavior
- whether broad permissions or personal-data collection would require stronger manual justification
- whether the app package gives too little static evidence to support its declared purpose

## vivo

Focus on:
- common domestic privacy baseline plus conservative review of sensitive permissions, privacy entry points, and denial handling
- whether listing description, screenshots, and app behavior are likely to align
- whether broad access requests would need explicit scenario explanation and runtime proof

## OPPO

Focus on:
- minimum-necessary permissions and data collection
- privacy-policy URL quality and accessibility
- higher-risk capabilities such as vpn, accessibility, device admin, overlay, install packages, or broad installed-app visibility
- copy or strings that suggest inducement, cheating, or hidden behavior
